About ORBIS

A 3D Planetary Observatory

ORBIS is a web based 3D application. It lets you rotate, zoom, and inspect four bodies in our solar system - Earth, Mars, Jupiter, and the Moon and tweak the lighting and view to see them how you want.

Why planets?

I've always been interested in space and planets. I used to read about them because I couldn't quite imagine that they were real.

How it was built

The app uses Three.js to render WebGL scenes in the browser. The planets themselves were modelled in Blender, each one is a UV sphere with a texture map wrapped around it. The website loads them as GLB files and centres them automatically, so they all sit in the same place regardless of how I exported them.

The catalogue on the left switches between models. The controls on the right toggle wireframe, auto rotate, and the lighting.

What I learned

UV unwrapping was the bit I hadn't done before. It wasn't actually difficult once I understood what it was. You're telling Blender how a flat image should wrap onto a 3D surface. Once that clicked, the other three planets went quickly.

Tested in current versions of Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on desktop, and on mobile Safari and Chrome on Android.
