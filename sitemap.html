Interaction map

  • Catalogue panel (left) — click any specimen to load it on the stage
  • Stage (centre) — drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click model for a spin burst
  • Controls panel (right) — wireframe toggle, auto-rotate toggle, sun and ambient light sliders
  • Top navigation — switches between content pages
